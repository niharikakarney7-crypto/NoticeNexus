The Current Campus Struggle

Students face high friction and miss vital opportunities:

  • ⚠️ Scattered channels: Key deadlines buried in noisy chat forwards.
  • ⚠️ Unverified rumors: Ambiguous requirements causing panic.
  • ⚠️ Language barriers: Complex academic jargon with no regional or audio support.
  • ⚠️ Missed deadlines: Scholarship and exam forms closing without notice.

The NoticeNexus Standard

Engineered for clarity, accountability, and student success:

  • ✅ 100% Source-Grounded: Direct links to official college sources.
  • ✅ Notice-to-Action Assistant: Clear summary of who, when, and what documents are required.
  • ✅ Universal Accessibility: One-click Text-to-Speech and Telugu language explanations.
  • ✅ Deadline Tracking: Interactive calendar and personal reminders.
